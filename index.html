Industries We Serve

Sector-specific expertise where integrity is non-negotiable

Every industry fails differently. We apply failure analysis and welding engineering to your sector’s codes, service conditions and risks.

01Oil & Gas

Pipelines, refineries, petrochemical plant and pressure equipment in demanding, corrosive service.

Failure Analysis
Corrosion, sulphide stress cracking, hydrogen damage and fatigue in pipelines, vessels and rotating equipment.
Welding & Joining
WPS/PQR evaluation to ASME IX and API 1104, repair-welding strategy and weld integrity for sour and high-pressure service.
Discuss an oil & gas challenge

02Defence

Armoured platforms, weapon systems and support equipment that must perform under shock, fatigue and extreme field conditions.

Failure Analysis
Root-cause investigation of field failures in high-hardness steels, castings and critical fasteners.
Welding & Joining
Armour-steel and high-strength weld procedures, hydrogen-cracking prevention and qualification to defence specifications.
Discuss a defence programme

03Aerospace

Flight-critical components where metallurgical uncertainty carries the highest consequence.

Failure Analysis
Fractography and metallurgical investigation of fatigue, creep and overload in titanium, nickel and aluminium alloys.
Welding & Joining
Procedure and qualification review to AWS D17.1, including GTAW, electron-beam and laser-welded joints.
Discuss an aerospace component

04Railways

Axles, wheels, bogies and rail welds that accumulate millions of load cycles in service.

Failure Analysis
Fatigue, rolling-contact and wear failure analysis, supported by ultrasonic and magnetic-particle NDT.
Welding & Joining
Bogie and car-body welding reviews to EN 15085 and RDSO requirements, plus rail-weld integrity assessment.
Discuss a railway asset

05Automotive & Transport

Vehicles, auto components and their supply chains — where durability failures drive warranty cost and recalls.

Failure Analysis
Durability and warranty-failure investigation of chassis, suspension and drivetrain components.
Welding & Joining
Production and robotic welding optimisation, weld-fatigue improvement and supplier process audits.
