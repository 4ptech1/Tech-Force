Industries We Serve

Built for sectors where failure is not an option

Our work is grounded in the compliance, NDT and metallurgical-reliability demands of India’s most safety-critical industries.

Sector 01Aerospace

Flight-critical parts leave no margin for metallurgical uncertainty. We support MRO shops, component manufacturers and their suppliers with failure investigations on rotating and structural parts, weld qualification for aerospace alloys and reviews of precision-formed hardware.

Focus areas

  • Fractography
  • Ni & Ti alloys
  • AWS D17.1
  • NDT review

Sector 02Defence

Armoured hulls, weapon platforms and support equipment rely on high-strength steels and heavy weldments that must survive shock, fatigue and harsh field conditions. We help manufacturers and defence suppliers qualify processes and resolve field failures.

Focus areas

  • Armour-steel welding
  • Hydrogen cracking
  • Spec compliance
  • Field-failure RCA

Sector 03Railways

Axles, wheels, bogie frames and rail welds endure millions of load cycles. We investigate fatigue and wear failures, review welding of bogie and car-body structures, and support suppliers working to RDSO and Indian Railways specifications.

Focus areas

  • RDSO specs
  • EN 15085
  • Ultrasonic testing
  • Fatigue analysis
