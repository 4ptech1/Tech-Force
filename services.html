Material Failure Analysis

Know exactly why it failed — and how to stop it happening again

When a component cracks, wears, corrodes or fractures early, the cost goes far beyond the part itself. We carry out structured, evidence-based investigations to establish the failure mechanism and its root cause, then translate the findings into practical corrective actions.

Discuss a Failure

Investigation Scope

  • Visual & macro examination
  • Fractography (stereo & SEM)
  • Metallography & microstructure evaluation
  • Hardness & mechanical testing
  • Chemical composition verification
  • NDT — UT, MPI, DPT, RT & eddy current

Mechanisms We Diagnose

  • Fatigue & corrosion fatigue
  • Ductile & brittle overload
  • Stress corrosion cracking
  • Hydrogen embrittlement
  • Wear, fretting & galling
  • Heat-treatment & material defects

What You Receive

  • Root-cause analysis report with photo-documented evidence
  • Corrective & preventive action (CAPA) recommendations
  • Material and specification review
  • Technical support for warranty & supplier disputes

Reference Standards

  • ASTM E3 / E407
  • ASTM E384 / E18
  • ASTM A370
  • IS 1608
  • ASM failure-analysis practice
02

Welding Processes

Welds that qualify first time and perform in service

A weld is only as reliable as the procedure, the welder and the material behind it. We support fabricators and OEMs from procedure development and qualification through to production troubleshooting — across carbon and alloy steels, stainless steels, aluminium, titanium and nickel alloys.

Discuss a Welding Problem

Engineering Scope

  • WPS, PQR & WPQ development and review
  • Process selection — SMAW, GMAW, GTAW, SAW, FCAW
  • Filler metal & consumable selection
  • Preheat, interpass & PWHT recommendations
  • Distortion & residual-stress control
  • Inspection plans & NDT acceptance criteria

Problems We Solve

  • Hot, cold & hydrogen-induced cracking
  • Porosity, lack of fusion & incomplete penetration
  • HAZ hardening or softening
  • Repeated qualification test failures
  • Dissimilar-metal joint failures
  • Fatigue cracking at weld toes

What You Receive

  • Qualified or reviewed WPS / PQR documentation
  • Weld defect investigation reports
  • Shop-floor process audits & welder guidance
  • Fabrication & inspection procedure reviews

Reference Standards

  • ASME Section IX
  • AWS D1.1
  • AWS D17.1
  • EN ISO 15614
  • EN 15085
  • ISO 3834
03

Coining & Precision Forming

Tighter tolerances, longer die life, fewer rejects

Coining and precision forming demand exact control of material flow, tonnage and tooling to hold tight tolerances and fine surface detail. We help manufacturers improve yield, extend die life and bring new parts into stable production faster.

Discuss a Forming Process

Engineering Scope

  • Process feasibility & forming sequence design
  • Press tonnage & load calculations
  • Material selection & formability assessment
  • Die design review & tool-steel selection
  • Lubrication & surface-finish optimisation
  • Annealing & intermediate heat treatment

Problems We Solve

  • Incomplete fill or poor detail definition
  • Cracking, flash & surface defects
  • Premature die wear, chipping or cracking
  • Dimensional drift & springback
  • High rejection rates on new parts

What You Receive

  • Process & tooling review report
  • Die failure analysis & life-improvement plan
  • Recommended process parameter windows
  • Trial support & first-article evaluation

Materials We Work With

  • Carbon & alloy steels
  • Stainless steels
  • Copper & brass
  • Aluminium alloys
  • Tool steels

How We Work

A clear, four-step engagement

Every assignment follows the same disciplined path — so you always know what happens next and what you’ll receive.

  1. 01

    Initial Assessment

    We review the problem, drawings, service history and any failed parts or samples, then agree a clear scope, timeline and quotation.

    → Scope & quotation
  2. 02

    Field / Lab Testing

    On-site inspection and NDT where needed, followed by laboratory examination — fractography, metallography, hardness and chemistry.

    → Test data & evidence
  3. 03

    Root Cause Analysis

    We correlate the evidence with design, material, process and service conditions to establish the failure mechanism and its root cause.

    → Validated root cause
  4. 04

    Engineering Report

    A standards-referenced report with findings, root cause and prioritised corrective actions — plus a review meeting with your team.

    → Report & CAPA plan
