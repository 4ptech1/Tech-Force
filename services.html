Material Failure Analysis

Establish exactly why it failed — and what must change

An unexplained failure is a business risk: unplanned downtime, warranty exposure, safety questions and strained customer relationships. Our investigations combine field evidence, laboratory examination and engineering judgement to establish the governing failure mechanism and its root cause — then translate the findings into corrective actions your organisation can implement with confidence.

Brief Us on a Failure

Investigation Scope

  • Root-cause investigation & failure-mode assessment
  • Fractography (stereo & SEM)
  • Metallography & microstructural evaluation
  • Hardness, mechanical & chemical verification
  • NDT — UT, MPI, DPT, RT & eddy current
  • Service-condition & design review

Mechanisms We Diagnose

  • Fatigue & corrosion fatigue
  • Ductile & brittle overload
  • Stress corrosion & sulphide stress cracking
  • Hydrogen embrittlement & damage
  • Pitting, erosion & galvanic corrosion
  • Wear, fretting & creep

What You Receive

  • Root-cause report with photo-documented evidence
  • Executive summary for leadership decisions
  • Prioritised corrective & preventive actions (CAPA)
  • Technical support for warranty, insurance & supplier disputes

Reference Standards

  • ASTM E3 / E407
  • ASTM E384 / E18
  • ASTM A370
  • NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156
  • IS 1608
  • ASM failure-analysis practice
02

Joining & Welding Engineering

Weldments engineered for integrity — defects resolved at the source

Welded joints are where design intent, material behaviour and fabrication practice converge — and where many structural failures begin. We provide independent welding engineering across carbon, low-alloy and stainless steels, aluminium, titanium and nickel alloys: evaluating procedures, diagnosing defects and assuring the structural integrity of critical weldments.

Discuss a Welding Challenge

Engineering Scope

  • WPS, PQR & WPQ evaluation and development
  • Weld-defect root-cause analysis
  • Structural-integrity & fitness-for-purpose assessment of weldments
  • Filler, preheat, interpass & PWHT strategy
  • Distortion & residual-stress control
  • Advanced joining — orbital GTAW, laser, electron-beam, brazing & dissimilar metals

Problems We Solve

  • Hot, cold & hydrogen-induced cracking
  • Porosity, lack of fusion & incomplete penetration
  • HAZ hardening, softening & embrittlement
  • Repeated qualification test failures
  • Dissimilar-metal joint failures
  • Fatigue cracking at weld toes

What You Receive

  • Evaluated or qualified WPS / PQR documentation
  • Weld-defect investigation reports
  • Integrity assessments for critical weldments
  • Fabrication, inspection & supplier process audits

Reference Standards

  • ASME Section IX
  • API 1104
  • AWS D1.1
  • AWS D17.1
  • EN ISO 15614
  • EN 15085
  • ISO 3834
  • BS 7910

How We Work

A disciplined, four-stage engagement

A transparent methodology from first brief to final report, so every stakeholder knows what will be delivered — and when.

  1. 01

    Initial Assessment

    We review the failure history, drawings, specifications and samples, frame the technical question, and agree scope, timeline and fees.

    → Scope & proposal
  2. 02

    Field / Lab Testing

    Site inspection and NDT where warranted, followed by laboratory examination — fractography, metallography, hardness and chemistry.

    → Evidence base
  3. 03

    Root Cause Analysis

    We correlate the evidence against design, material, fabrication and service conditions to isolate the governing mechanism.

    → Validated root cause
  4. 04

    Engineering Report

    A standards-referenced report with an executive summary, findings and prioritised corrective actions — presented to your team.

    → Report & CAPA plan
