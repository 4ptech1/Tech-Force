Failure Analysis & Root Cause Analysis

Establish exactly why it failed — and what must change

An unexplained failure means downtime, warranty cost and safety risk. We investigate component, equipment, structural and weld failures to find the true mechanism — material, design, fabrication, welding or service — and the actions that stop it recurring.

Brief Us on a Failure

Investigation Scope

  • Component, structural & weld failure investigation
  • Fractography (stereo & SEM)
  • Metallography & microstructural evaluation
  • Hardness, mechanical & chemical verification
  • NDT — UT, MPI, DPT, RT & eddy current
  • Service-condition & design review

Mechanisms We Diagnose

  • Fatigue & corrosion fatigue
  • Ductile & brittle overload
  • Stress corrosion & sulphide stress cracking
  • Hydrogen embrittlement & damage
  • Pitting, erosion & galvanic corrosion
  • Wear, fretting & creep

What You Receive

  • Root-cause report with photo-documented evidence
  • Failure-prevention & reliability improvement plan
  • Prioritised corrective & preventive actions (CAPA)
  • Technical support for warranty, insurance & supplier disputes

Reference Standards

  • ASTM E3 / E407
  • ASTM E384 / E18
  • ASTM A370
  • NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156
  • IS 1608
  • ASM failure-analysis practice
02

Welding Engineering & Technology

Welds that qualify first time — and cost less to produce

We support fabricators and OEMs from procedure development to production — improving weld integrity, cutting defects and rework, and raising productivity across steels, stainless, aluminium, titanium and nickel alloys.

Discuss a Welding Challenge

Engineering Scope

  • WPS / PQR development, qualification & optimisation
  • Process selection & application engineering
  • Welding metallurgy, weldability & material compatibility
  • Structural integrity of weldments
  • Automation & robotic welding applications
  • Quality, productivity & cost optimisation

Problems We Solve

  • Hot, cold & hydrogen-induced cracking
  • Porosity, lack of fusion & incomplete penetration
  • HAZ hardening, softening & embrittlement
  • High rework & rejection rates
  • Dissimilar-metal joint failures
  • Fatigue cracking at weld toes

What You Receive

  • Evaluated or qualified WPS / PQR documentation
  • Weld-defect investigation reports
  • Integrity assessments for critical weldments
  • Fabrication, inspection & supplier process audits

Reference Standards

  • ASME Section IX
  • API 1104
  • AWS D1.1
  • AWS D17.1
  • EN ISO 15614
  • EN 15085
  • ISO 3834
  • BS 7910
